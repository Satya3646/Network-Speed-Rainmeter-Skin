### Steps

1. Download Rainmeter from the official site: [rainmeter.net](https://www.rainmeter.net/).  
2. Run the downloaded installer (`.exe`) and complete the setup.  
3. After Rainmeter is installed, download the `.rmskin` file included in this repository.  
4. Double-click the `.rmskin` file to install and load the skin.  
5. The skin should now appear on your desktop.

---

### After Installation (Recommended)

Position the skin on your desktop, then open the Rainmeter manager.  
Set the **Position** option to **On Desktop**, as shown below.  
This locks the skin to the desktop layer, which is the intended behavior.  
If you know what you're doing, you may adjust other position modes as needed.
